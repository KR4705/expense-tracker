## How to use
1. Open each source and inspect the screenshots on the original page.
2. Select the individual screen(s) that best demonstrate the pattern.
3. If rights allow, save screenshot files locally in their matching app folder, with source metadata. Do not upload copyrighted third-party images to a public repo without permission.
4. Refer to source pages during Claude design analysis; explain the *pattern to learn*, not pixels to copy.
5. Convert *approved* observations into numbered style-guide rules; then design tokens; then component specs.

## Shared guidance for Claude
- A visible primary call-to-action follows each expense-entry decision.
- Category options pair a consistent visual marker with a readable label.
- Support multi-category tags (including Unknown).
- Express progression with active/completed/current states, not color alone.
- Keep expense entry guided and light; reserve dense information for the dashboard.

No final style-guide values, screenshots or tokens are approved by this catalog.
