# Duolingo — Guided progression (REF-D01)

**Approved inspiration:** visually clear completed/current/upcoming steps and positive completion feedback.

**Use for:** add-expense step indicator, current-step emphasis and successful-save acknowledgement.

**Desired behavior:** the user always sees where they are and knows how far remains; progress is not communicated using color alone.

**Avoid:** childish aesthetics, rewards/streaks that distract from capturing an expense.

Source: https://blog.duolingo.com/new-duolingo-home-screen-design/
