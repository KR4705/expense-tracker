**Approved inspiration:** concise mobile financial summaries and readable mobile cards, charts and breakdowns.

**Use for:** expense home/dashboard, total spent, trends, category insights.

**Desired behavior:** lead with important numbers, then a few key insights, then optional detail. Do not double-count expenses merely because multiple tags apply.
